# Shahi Kaju Paneer Gravy / Methi Malai Matar Gravy

A rich, creamy, cashew-and-almond-based gravy. This versatile North Indian base can be finished as a Shahi Kaju Paneer or a creamy Methi Malai Matar, marrying sweet nuts with aromatic spices and fresh cream.

**Prep time:** 1 Hour (for soaking nuts)  
**Cook time:** 20 Minutes  
**Yield:** 3 to 4 Servings  

---

## Ingredients

### The Base Gravy (Paste)
* **2** Medium onions, chopped
* **3 to 4** Garlic cloves, chopped
* **1 handful** Cashew nuts (kaju)
* **1 handful** Almonds (badam)
* **2-3** Cinnamon sticks
* **1 tsp** Cumin seeds (jeera)
* **1 pinch** Hing (asafoetida)
* **1 tbsp** Oil

### The Spices
* **1/2 tsp** Turmeric powder
* **1 tsp** Red chilli powder (adjust to taste)
* **1.5 tsp** Kitchen King masala (plus extra to taste)
* **1/2 tsp** Cumin-coriander powder (or very little coriander powder)
* Salt to taste

### The Finishers
* **3 to 4 tsp** Sugar
* **1 tbsp** Kasuri methi (dried fenugreek leaves)
* **2-3 tbsp** Fresh cream
* Fresh coriander leaves, chopped (for garnish)

### The Main Add-ins (Choose Option A or B)
* **Option A (Shahi Kaju Paneer):** 200 g Paneer (cubed) and 1/2 cup green peas, sautéed
* **Option B (Methi Malai Matar):** 1 cup Fresh methi (fenugreek leaves) and 1/2 cup green peas, sautéed with a pinch of turmeric and cumin powder

---

## Instructions

1. **Sauté the Aromatics:** Heat oil in a pan. Add the chopped onions, garlic cloves, cinnamon sticks, cumin seeds, and a pinch of hing. Sauté until the onions are soft and slightly caramelized. Do not let the onions brown. Shut off the heat.
2. **Soak the Nuts:** Separately, soak a handful of cashews and almonds in hot water for about 1 hour.
3. **Make the Pastes:**
   * Blend the soaked cashews and almonds in a mixer with a little water to form a smooth, pasty consistency.
   * Blend the cooled sautéed onion and garlic mixture (remove cinnamon sticks if desired, or keep for flavor) in the mixer to make a smooth paste.
4. **Cook the Gravy Base:** Heat a little oil in a clean pan. Add the onion-garlic paste and sauté. Add the turmeric, red chilli powder, Kitchen King masala, and a small pinch of cumin-coriander powder. Sauté for a minute until fragrant.
5. **Add Nut Paste:** Let the mixture bubble slightly, then add the cashew-almond paste. Sauté the mixture well.
6. **Simmer the Gravy:** Add water to the pan to increase the volume and achieve your desired gravy consistency. Simmer gently and taste for seasoning.
7. **Sauté the Add-ins:** In a separate pan, sauté your chosen add-ins:
   * **For Paneer & Peas:** Sauté green peas and paneer cubes until slightly golden.
   * **For Methi & Matar:** Sauté green peas and fresh methi leaves with a pinch of turmeric and cumin powder.
8. **Combine and Finish:** Add the cooked add-ins to the simmering gravy. Stir well.
9. **Adjust Seasoning:** Taste and add extra Kitchen King masala, red chilli powder, or salt to adjust to your required taste.
10. **Add Herbs & Sweetness:** Crush the kasuri methi between your palms and drop it in. Add the chopped fresh coriander, and stir in the 3-4 teaspoons of sugar.
11. **Enrich with Cream:** Stir in the fresh cream, cook for 1 minute on low heat, and serve hot.
