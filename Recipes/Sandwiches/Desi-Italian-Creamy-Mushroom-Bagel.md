# The Desi-Italian Creamy Mushroom Bagel

A rich, no-roux fusion sandwich inspired by Chef Ranveer Brar. This recipe swaps heavy flour pastes for a quick cream-and-cheese reduction, marrying earthy Indian street-food aromatics with a hint of Italian herbs, all served on a crispy Everything Bagel.

**Prep time:** 10 Minutes  
**Cook time:** 12 Minutes  
**Yield:** 2 to 3 Sandwiches  

---

## Ingredients

### The Produce
* **225 g** Baby Bella mushrooms, chopped
* **1 whole** Green bell pepper, chopped
* **1.5 tbsp (approx. 20 g)** Fresh ginger, finely chopped
* **1.5 tbsp (approx. 20 g)** Fresh garlic, finely chopped
* **2** Green chillies, finely chopped
* **2 wedges** Fresh lemon

### The Dairy (The No-Roux Base)
* **30 g** Butter
* **240 ml** Half & Half
* **4 slices** Processed cheese *(plus 2-3 extra slices for sandwich assembly)*

### The Spices
* **2 tsp** Kasuri methi (dried fenugreek leaves)
* **1 tsp** Chilli flakes
* **2 generous pinches** Mixed Italian seasoning (or oregano)
* **1/2 tsp** Black pepper
* Salt to taste

### The Bread
* **2 to 3** Everything Bagels, halved

---

## Instructions

1. **Sear the Mushrooms:** Heat a large skillet over medium-high heat and melt the 30 g of butter. Add the chopped baby Bella mushrooms. Do not add salt yet. Fry undisturbed for 2 to 3 minutes until they release their initial moisture and develop a deep, golden-brown sear.
2. **Sauté the Aromatics:** Toss in the ginger, garlic, green chillies, and green bell pepper. Sauté everything together for about 2 minutes to cook out the raw bite of the garlic and ginger while retaining a slight crunch in the peppers.
3. **Reduce the Cream:** Turn the heat down to medium-low. Pour in the 240 ml of Half & Half. Simmer gently for 3 to 4 minutes. As the liquid reduces, it will thicken naturally and begin to coat the vegetables.
4. **Emulsify the Cheese:** Tear the 4 cheese slices into pieces and drop them into the pan. Stir continuously. The emulsifiers in the cheese will melt into the warm cream, binding it into a thick, glossy, cohesive white sauce.
5. **Season the Filling:** Turn off the heat completely. Add the black pepper, chilli flakes, and Italian seasoning. Vigorously crush the Kasuri methi between your palms and drop it in. Stir well, taste the mixture, and add salt only if needed.
6. **Brighten with Citrus:** Let the pan sit off the heat for 60 seconds to stop the bubbling. Stir in a squeeze of fresh lemon juice from your wedges. Doing this off the heat ensures the acidity brightens the heavy cream without curdling the dairy.
7. **Toast and Waterproof:** Toast the Everything Bagels until very crispy. Immediately place an extra cheese slice onto the hot bottom half of each bagel. The residual heat will melt the cheese, creating a waterproof barrier to protect the bread from getting soggy.
8. **Assemble and Serve:** Spoon the hot, creamy mushroom and pepper filling heavily onto the cheese layers. Place the top halves of the bagels on the filling, press down gently to lock them together, and serve immediately.
